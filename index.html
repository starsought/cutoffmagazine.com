ABOUT THE MAGAZINE

CUTOFF is published by JUNE B.D., a multidisciplinary artist currently based in Maine, with the help of friends and contributors. Issue 1 was published on June 21, 2026, and future issues will be published biannually, on the summer and winter solstices. The eventual goal is to publish quarterly, with smaller issues on the spring and fall equinoxes.

Contact the editor at junebd@pm.me for questions, comments, and contributions.
